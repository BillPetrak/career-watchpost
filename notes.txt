How to resume working on CareerOS

Open a terminal and run:

cd ~/Desktop/Projects/CareerOS

Then synchronize the environment (this is safe even if nothing changed):

uv sync

Now launch OpenCode:

opencode



Ctrl+L

Many terminal applications use this to clear and redraw the current screen. It may immediately remove the floating characters.
